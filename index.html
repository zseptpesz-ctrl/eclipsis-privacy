Privacy Policy — Eclipsis

Last updated: 23/09/2026

1. Analytics

Eclipsis does not currently use any third-party analytics service. We do not track your individual usage of the app.

2. Data Processed and Purposes

Eclipsis does not operate any server of its own. No data is transmitted to or stored on any infrastructure we control. Two categories of data exist:

a) Data stored only on your device
Your habits, progress, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device using SwiftData (Apple). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. We have no access to it.
Legal basis: performance of the service you requested (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

b) Data processed by our providers to manage subscriptions
When you make a purchase or subscribe, Apple (App Store / StoreKit) and RevenueCat process, for their own technical purposes, transaction-related data: anonymous purchase identifier, purchase history, subscription status. We do not have access to your payment card details, which are handled exclusively by Apple.
Legal basis: performance of the subscription contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

3. Data Recipients

- Apple Inc. (payment processing)
- RevenueCat, Inc. (subscription management)
We do not sell or share your data for marketing or commercial purposes.

4. Transfers Outside the European Union

RevenueCat and Apple are companies based in the United States. Purchase-related data may therefore be processed outside the European Union, under the safeguards provided by these providers (standard contractual clauses).

5. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the app or its data. Subscription-related data is retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own policies.

6. Your Rights

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL or your local data protection authority.
